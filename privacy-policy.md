Privacy Policy
==============

In Short
--------
Homilia Messenger doesn't log or share your personal information.

**We don't track you. We don't profile you.**

Data you enter or that is collected towards the functionality of the app is saved locally within the application folder, on your device only.

The app talks only to the services you choose, which by default are [available to download] local models only.

Contact photos you generate are made on your device from your description, which is never sent anywhere. Downloading an image model, or looking one up to add it, contacts Hugging Face only to read the repository's information and files.

If you turn on web search in Settings, contacts may send short search queries, made from your latest message with names and contact details removed, to [Tavily](https://tavily.com) using an API key you provide. Your conversations, memories, and profile are never sent. Web search is off by default, and the API key is kept in your operating system's credential store.
